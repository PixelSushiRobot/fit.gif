→ Tuned for 2D illustration & pixel art loops

  • Cut colors first. Flat cel art and pixel art use narrow palettes — 64 colors is usually invisible, 32 is fine for two-tone loops. This is the biggest win here, not resize.
  • Then drop frames. A 24fps loop trimmed to every-2nd-frame plays at ~12fps and roughly halves size with no perceptible change.
  • Resize last. Only shrink dimensions when colors + frames aren't enough — most feeds display GIFs small anyway.
  • Pick your art type. "Pixel art (crisp)" disables smoothing so pixels stay sharp; "Illustration" uses high-quality smooth downscaling. Neither dithers, which keeps flat color areas clean.
  • Still too big? If the platform accepts video (X and Farcaster do), an MP4 export of the same loop is usually far smaller than any GIF.
